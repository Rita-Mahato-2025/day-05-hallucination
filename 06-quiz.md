### ** 3 Quiz Questions**

** Q1. In the story, Robo said there were exactly 7,42,891 stars in the universe with 100% confidence. What is this called?**

1. A deliberate joke by the AI
2. A software update
3. A verified fact from a reliable source
4. A hallucination: an answer that sounds true but is made up

** Correct Ans : 4. A hallucination: an answer that sounds true but is made up**

Spot on! A hallucination is when AI gives an answer that sounds correct but is false or invented. Robo generated a likely-sounding number without verifying it.

** Q2. Why do AI hallucinations happen?**

1. AI always copies answers from one website
2. AI is trying to trick the user
3. AI only works when the internet is slow
4. AI predicts likely words and answers, and does not automatically verify every fact

** Correct Ans : 4. AI predicts likely words and answers, and does not automatically verify every fact**

Exactly right! AI generates what is likely to sound right based on patterns. It doesn't check each claim against reality, so a confident tone says nothing about accuracy.

** Q3. You ask AI for a report and it gives you a very specific statistic and a citation. What is the best next step?**

1. Open the source, compare the facts, and verify before using it
2. Use it as is, because it sounds confident and specific
3. Delete the whole report and never use AI again
4. Ask the AI if it is sure and trust a 'yes'

** Correct Ans : 1. Open the source, compare the facts, and verify before using it.**

Great call! Exact numbers, dates, names and citations are high-risk areas for hallucination. Asking the AI to confirm itself isn't verification. Draft with AI, then verify before you trust.
