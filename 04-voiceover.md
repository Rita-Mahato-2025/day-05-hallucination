### **Voiceover Script: Day 5, The Robot That Almost Won the Science Fair**
**Voice cast**
**Role**	**Voice style**
**Narrator**	Warm, storyteller tone, like reading to kids at bedtime. Medium pace.
**Aarav**	Young, curious, energetic boy. Gets quieter and softer after the truth comes out.
**Mia**	Bright, quick, slightly sassy girl. Skeptical but caring.
**Robo**	Clear, slightly robotic but friendly. Very confident in Scene 1, hesitant in Scene 3.
**Ms. Sen**	Calm, kind, gentle adult voice. Never scolds.
**Rita (Scene 6)**	Your own voice. Warm, clear, a little slower for the takeaways.

**Tip:** If you use an AI voice tool (ElevenLabs, etc.), generate each character's lines separately, then place them on the timeline.

### **Scene 1: The Hook (0:00–0:10)**

**MIA** (out of breath, urgent)
"Stop! Don't submit your project!"

**AARAV** (startled)
"What happened? Robo checked everything!"

**MIA** (pointing, dramatic)
"Your poster says there are exactly seven lakh, forty-two thousand, eight hundred ninety-one stars. Ms. Sen says nobody knows the exact number!"

**AARAV** (turning, disbelief)
"Robo! You told me it was correct!"

**ROBO** (proud, flat, super confident)
"Of course. I am one hundred percent confident."

**NARRATOR** (low, mysterious)
"Could the smartest robot in school... have made it up?"

### **Scene 2: The Setup (0:10–0:25)**

**NARRATOR** (warm, flashback tone)
"Two weeks earlier, Aarav built Robo for the science fair. It answered everything."

**AARAV** (excited)
"Who invented the telephone?"

**ROBO** (cheerful)
"Alexander Graham Bell!"

**AARAV**
"Why do plants need sunlight?"

**ROBO** (cheerful)
"To make food through photosynthesis!"

**NARRATOR** (slower, a hint of warning)
"Every answer sounded right. So Aarav... stopped checking."

**AARAV** (casual, not thinking)
"Robo, how many stars are in the universe?"

**ROBO** (very confident)
"Exactly seven lakh, forty-two thousand, eight hundred ninety-one!"

### **Scene 3: The Confrontation (0:25–0:45)**

**MIA** (arms crossed, skeptical)
"Did you check it?"

**AARAV** (defensive)
"Why would I? Robo knows everything!"

**MS. SEN** (gentle, kind)
"AI doesn't always know what's true. It predicts what answer sounds right. Sometimes, it makes things up."

**AARAV** (small, hurt, turning to Robo)
"Then why did you sound so sure?"

**ROBO** (pause, then quieter and hesitant)
"I generated the answer because it seemed likely... I did not verify it."

**NARRATOR** (soft, reflective)
"Aarav had trusted the answer because it sounded smart. Not because it was checked."

### **Scene 4: The Resolution (0:45–1:10)**

**NARRATOR** (upbeat, determined, picking up pace)
"Aarav had only one hour. He didn't give up. He became a fact detective."

**MS. SEN** (warm, proud smile in voice)
"Now you're doing real science. You're not collecting answers. You're checking them."

**JUDGE** (friendly, curious, new voice)
"How did you make sure your information was correct?"

**AARAV** (confident, proud)
"Robo helped me with the first draft. But I checked the facts myself. AI can hallucinate, which means giving answers that sound true... but aren't."

**NARRATOR** (slow, meaningful, emphasize the last line)
"Aarav didn't win because his robot knew everything. He won because he knew... when not to trust it."

### **Scene 5: The Ending (1:10–1:20)**

**NARRATOR** (gentle, warm)
"That evening, Aarav gave Robo a new rule."

**ROBO** (reading aloud, friendly, a little humble)
"I can help you find answers. But let's check them together!"

**MIA** (laughing)
"Looks like your robot finally learned something."

**AARAV** (grinning)
"Actually... I did."

### **Scene 6: The Takeaway (1:20–1:30)**

**RITA** (your voice, clear, steady, slightly slower)

"Day 5 takeaway.

Hallucination is when AI gives an answer that sounds right, but is made up.

It happens because AI predicts likely words. It doesn't verify facts.

Be extra careful with exact numbers, dates, names, citations, and recent events.

Ask for sources. Open them. Compare. Get a second opinion.

Draft with AI. Verify before you trust.

The smartest person in the room isn't the one who believes every answer. It's the one who knows how to check.

See you on Day 6!"
