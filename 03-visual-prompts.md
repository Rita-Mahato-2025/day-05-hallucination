### **Tips for consistency**
Reuse the first image as a reference (image-to-video) so characters stay identical.
Generate Scenes 1 to 5 as stills first, approve them, then animate.
Add the text overlays in your editor (CapCut or Canva) instead of generating them. AI tools often misspell on-screen text, including the "7,42,891 STARS" poster and Robo's screen.
Add the voiceover separately. Generate clips silent, then record or add the dialogue on top.
